2. About the App

2.1 Features

忘言 is an open-source, local-first e-book reader. Its main features include:

  • Reading EPUB, TXT and PDF e-books, and managing a local library, reading progress, bookmarks, notes and reading statistics.
  • Importing files or authorizing folders through the system file picker so that books in the locations you designate can be read directly.
  • Editing TXT inside the App and writing the changes back to the file you authorized when you confirm saving.
  • System TTS listening; you may also configure OpenAI TTS, Xiaomi MiMo or a custom compatible third-party TTS service yourself.
  • Parsing PDFs through the MinerU/OpenDataLab cloud service after you enable it.
  • Syncing books, reading progress, bookmarks and notes over WebDAV after you configure it.
  • Translating selected text through Xiaomi MiMo, DeepSeek, OpenAI or a custom compatible service after you configure it and enable or trigger it manually.
  • Downloading dictionary packs on demand from “Settings → Services → Local dictionary”; after installation lookups are offline and nothing is downloaded or updated automatically.
  • Turning selected text, notes or bookmarks into excerpt cards that you may save to the system photo library or share with other apps.
  • Looking up online covers through search services such as Bing, Baidu or Google when you use that feature, and downloading remote fonts on demand when you choose the Fangsong typeface.

2.2 Nature of the App

  • Local reading, local parsing and system TTS do not upload book content by default.
  • WebDAV, MinerU cloud parsing, third-party TTS and AI translation are all optional network features that require you to configure, enable or confirm them.
  • The App offers no 忘言 account registration or sign-in, and no developer-operated cloud library.
  • Local dictionary lookups happen on the device; the queried word, book text and filter hits are never uploaded.
  • The original 忘言 code is released under the GNU GPLv3 license; third-party components and adapted code follow their own licenses.
